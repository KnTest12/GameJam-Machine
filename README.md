# ROGUE.EXE

> *A grid-based combat game built for Gamedev.js Jam 2026 — Theme: MACHINES*

---

## 🕹️ About

You are **UNIT_01** — an AI deployed into a virtual network to hunt down and terminate a rogue AI that has gone beyond its parameters. Fight through the 10 layers of data clusters and defeat the rogue AI.

**Inspired by:** Mega Man Battle Network / One Step From Eden

---

## 🎮 Controls

| Input | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Arrow Keys | Move (grid-based) |
| Space | Shoot |

---

## ⚔️ Gameplay

- Navigate a **4x4 grid** on the player side
- Shoot enemies on the **enemy side** to destroy them
- **Dodge highlighted tiles**
- Clear all enemies to advance to the next level
- Survive all 9 data clusters and face the **Rogue AI Core**

---

## 👾 Enemy Types

| Enemy | Behavior |
|-------|----------|
| **Turret** | Targets your current tile |
| **Coil** | Attacks your entire row |
| **Bomb** | Random cross pattern attack |
| **Nova** | X pattern centered on your position |
| **Mobile** | Moves vertically, fires a shockwave across your row based on the position of the mobile enemy |
| **Zone Denial** | Alternates row and column attacks, long telegraphic attacks that set tiles to deal constant damage |
| **Rogue AI Core** | Boss — two phases, phase 1: familiar attack patterns in order, phase 2: random attack pattern, positional based |
